1. Objective and Setup
Kill the opponent's King before they kill yours. Each player has one King. If your King dies, you lose immediately unless
an effect saves it, such as Second Life.

Setup
      Shuffle the Character Card deck and place 5 face-down Character Cards on each player's side.
      The middle card on each side is that player's King. The King spot is known, but the Character Card remains face
       down.
      Do not look at face-down Character Cards, including your own King.
      Shuffle the Power Card deck. Each player draws 3 Power Cards.
      Use rock, paper, scissors to decide who goes first.



2. Board, Turns, Battles, and Kings
Board and Direction
The standard board has 10 spaces in one continuous loop: Y1 -> Y2 -> Y3 -> Y4 -> Y5 -> A5 -> A4 -> A3 -> A2 -> A1 ->
back to Y1. Each player begins with 5 spaces on their side. Y3 and A3 are the King starting spots. Characters follow the
board arrows when they move forward.

Main Actions
      Move Forward: Move one character forward one space if that next space is empty.
      Attack: If an enemy is directly in front of your character, attack using attacker ATK vs defender DEF. If the attacker
       wins, the defender dies and the attacker moves into the defeated character's space.
      Self-Defend: If an enemy is directly behind your character, self-defend using DEF vs DEF. If the self-defending
       character wins, it stays in its space and the loser dies.

“Directly in front,” “directly behind,” and “directly next to” mean the immediately adjacent board spaces in the normal
board path. Nexus Tree and Bridge connections do not change what counts as directly next to.

Revealing and Abilities
   A Character Card is revealed when it enters battle. Once revealed, it stays face up.
   Character abilities only work after that character is revealed.
   When a character wins or loses, apply the card text and battle result before moving on.

Ties
   A normal tie kills both battling characters.
   King Tie Advantage: if a King ties a non-King, the King wins the tie.
   Final King Duel: if both players only have Kings left, they immediately battle using King ATK vs King ATK. If that ties
    after Power Cards, the game is a draw.

Kings Crossing Territory Lines
Whenever your King crosses into enemy territory, draw 1 Power Card. This can happen by moving or by winning an
attack that moves the King across the line. A side card may count as its creator for line-crossing only when its own card
text specifically says so.



3. Attachments, Power Cards, Flying, and Sessions
Graveyard and Used Power Cards
   Dead Character Cards go to the Graveyard.
   Used Power Cards go to the Used Power Card pile.
   If a character dies with attached Power Cards, such as Weapons or Vehicles, those cards go to the Used Power
    Card pile unless their card says otherwise.
   If a character dies with attached Character Cards, those attached Character Cards go to the Graveyard.
   A revived character does not regain old attachments unless a card says otherwise.

Attachments
Attachments stay with a character when it moves, swaps, or dies. Attachments can include Weapons, Vehicles, Horses,
Apprentices, Followers, merged bread characters, and other card effects. A character may ride 1 Horse at a time. A
Horse may ride a Horse.

Power Card Timing
Always follow the timing printed on the card. Multiple Power Cards may be used in the same battle unless a card prevents
it. Unless a card says permanent, a stat change lasts only for the current battle or turn.

Flying and Falling
   A character with a Flying Vehicle still follows the board arrows for normal movement.
   A flying character may move onto an occupied space by flying above the character already there.
   A flying character above an enemy may attack the character below using ATK vs ATK; the character below cannot
    attack upward.
   A flying character directly in front of an enemy may be attacked normally.
   A flying character above a teammate may combine stats with that teammate when battling from the shared space. If
    stacked teammates start a battle and lose, the character that started the battle dies. If an enemy attacks the stack
    and wins, the attacker chooses which character dies.
   If a character is above another character and loses its Flying Vehicle, it falls and dies.

Multi-Game Session Rule
When playing multiple games in one session, cards used in finished games go into a Used pile and are not available for
later games in that session. Search effects can only search unused cards unless a card says otherwise. Thanos
collecting Infinity Stones is a special exception as written on Thanos and Infinity Gauntlet.

6. Later Official Power Cards, Locations, Vehicles, and Side Cards
POWER OUTAGE

  Permanent negative attachment. The attached revealed character cannot use its special ability. It may be played immediately
  as that character declares the ability to cancel it. Disarm can remove it.
ROTATE & RESET

  Rotate the board 180 degrees. Control characters now on your side. If there is no King, choose a character on your side to
  become King. Both sides must have at least 1 character.
CREDIT CARD

  Spend cards from the Used Power pile, each for +1 ATK or DEF in battle. Spent cards are set aside. At Locations, spend
  Used Power Cards for a reward costing that Location's ATK.
TRUST THE DECK

  During battle, reveal the top Power Card. If legal, play it immediately. If not, your battling character gets -3 ATK / -3 DEF for
  that battle. Discard the revealed card.
KICK-OUT!!

  During battle, if your character is losing, make the stat currently being used equal the opponent's equivalent stat. Battle
  continues.
CATAPULT

  On landing, reveal the bottom Character Card and launch it forward using that card's ATK. The revealed card goes to the
  Graveyard. If it lands on a non-King, that character dies. If it lands on a King, the launched character dies. King on King is a
  Final Duel. If it lands on an open spot, it does not die.
GYM

  First character to land may do 10 push-ups to draw 1 Power Card. Then remove Gym.
LOOKOUT TOWER

  Permanent Location. A character landing there may secretly look at 1 face-down Character or 1 random opponent Power
  Card.
SWITCH MARKET

  First character to land may swap places with any character. Then remove Switch Market.
