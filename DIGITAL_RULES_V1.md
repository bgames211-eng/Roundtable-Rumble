Roundtable Rumble

BOARD PATH:
Y1 → Y2 → Y3 → Y4 → Y5 → A5 → A4 → A3 → A2 → A1 → Y1

Y1–Y5 are one player’s territory.
A1–A5 are the other player’s territory.
Y3 and A3 are the King starting spaces.

Every character’s forward direction must use that exact loop.
Do not use screen direction, left/right, or team side to decide forward or backward.

KING TERRITORY DRAW:
Whenever a King changes position from its own territory to the opponent’s territory, draw exactly 1 Power Card.

This applies regardless of how the King moved, including:

- normal forward movement
- backward movement
- winning an attack and moving into the defeated character’s space
- Portal
- Space Stone
- Nightcrawler-style teleportation
- any other legal card effect or ability that relocates the King
The draw is based only on:

1. the King’s controller,
2. the territory of the King’s starting space, and
3. the territory of the King’s ending space.
A King does not draw when:

- moving within its own territory
- moving within enemy territory
- leaving enemy territory and returning to its own territory
- failing to move because it lost or tied a battle
- being moved from one enemy-territory space to another
- being moved from one own-territory space to another
Each completed movement effect can trigger a maximum of 1 King Territory Draw.
